### Logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: finished project logo and 3:2 landscape thumbnail for the EffectsHack hackathon submission.
Primary request: create an original, memorable logo for EffectsHack, a beginner-friendly guitar effects processor that turns a song reference into a playable effects-rack tone.
Scene/backdrop: solid deep charcoal #12171d, matching the actual application. No photographed objects or interface mockups.
Subject: a strong, compact guitar-pick emblem with a clean audio-waveform cutout; warm amber-orange #ffa657 as the primary mark, with a restrained mint-teal #69d5ba signal accent. The symbol should feel like music equipment and signal processing.
Style/medium: precise flat vector-like brand design, minimal, scalable, crisp edges, balanced negative space. Refined and approachable.
Composition/framing: one centered emblem above one centered wordmark, generous breathing room, cohesive single logo lockup, landscape 3:2 composition. Make the mark legible at small thumbnail sizes.
Text (verbatim): "EffectsHack". Spell it E-f-f-e-c-t-s-H-a-c-k. Modern clean geometric sans-serif lettering in off-white #edf2f6, with thoughtful spacing. Exact name once only.
Constraints: only the symbol and wordmark on the charcoal background. No tagline, no extra words, no UI panels, no photographs, no 3D, no gradients, no glow, no drop shadows, no watermark, no grid of variations.

### Final transparent emblem prompt

Use case: logo-brand, transparent app icon. Use the attached EffectsHack logo as the design reference. Recreate only its guitar-pick pedal emblem on a completely transparent square background, without the wordmark. A bold smooth amber-orange guitar-pick silhouette with three solid deep-charcoal circular knob faces across the top, short orange knob pointers, one solid deep-charcoal rounded rectangular panel in the middle carrying a continuous mint-teal audio waveform, and one deep-charcoal outlined round footswitch near the bottom. Render all internal dark details as opaque deep charcoal, NOT transparent holes. Only the area outside the pick is transparent. Preserve the original emblem's composition and proportions. Crisp clean flat colors, smooth antialiased edges, no speckles, no texture, no distressing, no yellow fringes, no gradients, no extra colored pixels outside the silhouette. Centered mark with generous clear margins. No text, no glow, no shadow, no background, no mockup.
